i iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the link in Safari (the camera opens it there by itself).
  2. Sign in, so the app opens on your calendar next time.
  3. Tap Share — the square with the arrow.
  4. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

A Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Sign in, so the app opens on your calendar next time.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  4. Choose Install app or Add to home screen.

What you can do there

  • See what is free — your own bookings by name, everyone else's time simply as busy.
  • Book by the hour — pick a free slot, name it, done. It is yours straight away.
  • Take whole days — give it a date range and every open day in it is booked for a longer project.
  • Cancel what you no longer need — one tap, and the time is free for everyone again.
You need an invitation from us to sign in. If the link has expired, write to us and we will send a new one. Bookings reach our studio diary the moment you make them — nothing else to confirm.
